# Leafspan development authority

- **Apple icon canvas rule:** provide an unmasked, square, full-bleed opaque background. Apple owns the outer rounded-corner/squircle mask. Never bake a rounded tile, transparent outer margin, perimeter stroke or outer tile shadow into the source. The inner apricot notebook is foreground artwork and may have its own rounded folds. This pipeline exports RGB PNGs and rejects transparent/pre-cut source artwork. See [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) and [Icon Composer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/creating-your-app-icon-using-icon-composer).
