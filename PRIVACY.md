# PKS Notes Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 7, 2026**

This policy applies to the macOS app **PKS Notes** (`com.moretea.pks.notes`).

## Summary

PKS Notes is a local-first Markdown and PDF workspace. The developer does not operate a PKS Notes account system, analytics service, advertising service, telemetry backend, or custom cloud sync service.

## Notes and files

PKS Notes reads and writes only folders and files that you choose to open. Access is provided through the macOS App Sandbox and security-scoped permissions.

The developer does not receive or store the contents of your Markdown notes, PDFs, MindNode files, filenames, or local file paths.

If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive, synchronization is provided by Apple and is governed by your Apple/iCloud settings and Apple's policies. PKS Notes does not operate a separate sync server.

## Optional local PKS integration

PKS Notes can connect to an optional Personal Knowledge System service at `127.0.0.1` on the same Mac for local search and related-knowledge features. This connection uses the local loopback interface; PKS Notes does not send those requests to a developer-operated server.

The app remains usable for its core Markdown, file-management, and PDF features when that optional local service is unavailable.

## Purchases

PKS Notes Pro is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase handled by Apple's StoreKit.

Apple provides product information, localized pricing, purchase processing, transaction verification data, and restore services. PKS Notes verifies Apple transactions on the device to determine whether Pro is unlocked. The developer does not operate a receipt-validation server and does not receive your Apple Account credentials or payment-card information.

Apple's own privacy terms apply to App Store and payment processing.

## External links

If you choose to open a web source in your browser, the destination website and your browser may process data under their own privacy policies. PKS Notes does not control those external services.

## Data retention

Because PKS Notes has no developer-operated account or application backend, the developer has no PKS Notes user profile, note database, or cloud copy to retain or delete.

Your documents remain in the locations you selected. App preferences and sandbox access bookmarks are stored locally by the app/macOS and can be removed by deleting the app's local data.

## Support and privacy questions

For support or privacy questions, use the public support channel described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

Do not post private note contents, credentials, tokens, or sensitive local paths in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities or accidental data exposure should be reported through the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
